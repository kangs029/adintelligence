📌 Assumptions & Notes

  • Bedrock is the dominant cost — every Ad Analyst query consumes ~3,000 input tokens + ~500 output tokens (includes system prompt, tool definitions, and artifact data slice)
  • Amplify free tier covers all hosting for both demo and light production scenarios (500K SSR requests, 100 GB-hours/month free)
  • Lambda runs entirely within the perpetual free tier (1M free requests/month, 400K GB-sec/month)
  • EventBridge Scheduler: 14 million free invocations/month — pipeline runs (max 15/month) cost $0.00
  • All prices sourced from AWS official pricing pages · August 2026
  • Prices shown are for ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) except Bedrock which is us-east-1 — both regions have identical pricing for these services
  • Costs scale linearly with Ad Analyst usage: each 1,000 additional queries adds ~$16.50 to monthly Bedrock cost
